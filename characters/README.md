# Characters

Reference sheets for the podcast's two on-screen characters. Attach both sheets to ChatGPT together with prompt 3 ([prompts/3-settings-and-images.md](../prompts/3-settings-and-images.md)), so every scene image shows the same faces, hair and outfits. The app's Script step offers both sheets as downloads next to that prompt.

## Shilpa
- **Look:** Indian teenage girl (15–17), long wavy dark-brown hair, warm smile. She wears a dusty-pink knit sweater with blue jeans.
- **Palette:** dusty pink, light blue, cream, navy, dark brown.
- **Props:** black over-ear headphones, black podcast microphone, silver laptop, pink/yellow/blue books, pink water bottle ("Good Vibes Only"), small potted plant.
- **Personality:** casual, smart, approachable. She speaks simple, clear, friendly English.
- **Expressions on the sheet:** greeting, curious, surprised, thinking, laughing, explaining, listening, agreeing, encouraging, signing off.

## Arjun
- **Look:** Indian teenage boy (15–17), short curly black hair, friendly smile. He wears an open denim shirt over a white T-shirt.
- **Palette:** navy, light blue, cream, tan, dark brown.
- **Props:** black over-ear headphones, black podcast microphone, silver laptop, stacked books, pencil holder, blue mug.
- **Personality:** casual, smart, relatable. He speaks simple, clear, student-friendly English.
- **Expressions on the sheet:** greeting, curious, surprised, thinking, laughing, explaining, listening, agreeing, encouraging, signing off.

## Shared setting cues
Both sheets use the same home studio: light wooden bookshelves, potted plants, warm daylight, and a podcast microphone on a boom arm. Reuse it for the main studio scene so episodes feel like one show.
